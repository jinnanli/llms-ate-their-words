  Final aggregate results, uncertainty summaries, and paper-facing number
  lookups for the four research questions.

results/cross_rq/ and results/restoration/
  Compact final supporting analyses retained because they delimit the reported
  causal interpretation.

results/figures/
  Final vector PDFs. PNG previews are omitted because they are redundant.

Intentionally excluded for size, privacy, or licensing:
  raw model generations and conversation transcripts; API caches and response
  IDs; model checkpoints and Hugging Face caches; full layer/head attention
  tensors; third-party benchmark corpora and Spider databases; logs and cluster
  job output; preliminary/pilot/obsolete analyses; failed annotation artifacts;
  manuscript sources/PDFs; Git metadata; editor and agent files; planning notes;
  old figures; archives; personal paths; and credentials.
